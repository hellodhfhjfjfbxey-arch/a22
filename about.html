The maison

A whole website about the finishing touch.

Hosiery is an independent website about tights and legwear.

We explain denier, colour, fit and care, and show how tights finish an outfit. Our denier scale and size finder make it easy to choose the right pair.

We do not sell tights, use affiliate links or accept payment for mentions. Every guide is reviewed by a second editor and dated.
