Tights Care & Fit

Well-fitting tights feel better and last longer. Good care does the rest.

iFinding your size

Use the brand’s chart, which usually plots height against weight. If you are between sizes, size up for comfort and length; tights that are too short pull down at the waist and wear through faster.

iiPutting them on

  1. Remove rings and check your nails for rough edges.
  2. Gather one leg down to the toe.
  3. Slip your foot in and roll the leg up slowly.
  4. Repeat, then ease the fabric up evenly.

iiiWashing gently

Hand wash in cool water with a mild detergent, or put tights in a mesh laundry bag on a delicate cycle. Avoid fabric softener, which can weaken elastane, and never wring them out.

ivDrying and storing

Dry flat or hang by the waistband, away from radiators and direct sun. Store them folded in a soft pouch or a drawer with no rough edges, separate from hooks and zips.

vSmall snags and ladders

A dab of clear nail varnish at the end of a small ladder stops it running in an emergency. Opaque tights can often be worn a little longer; sheer pairs are best retired once they ladder.
